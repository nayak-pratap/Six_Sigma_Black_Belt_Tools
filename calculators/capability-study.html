Capability Study: Cp, Cpk, Pp, Ppk

Separate short-term and long-term capability from subgrouped data, with Cp, Cpk, Pp, Ppk, Cpm, expected PPM and a histogram against the specification.

Within-subgroup σ = R̄ ÷ d2 (or MR̄ ÷ 1.128 for individuals): potential capability, Cp and Cpk. Overall σ is the standard deviation of all values: performance, Pp and Ppk. A big gap between Cpk and Ppk means the process drifts between subgroups. Cpm penalises being off target. Needs a stable, roughly normal process, so check a control chart first. Example: bore diameter (mm), 20 subgroups of 5, USL 10.35, LSL 9.65.
