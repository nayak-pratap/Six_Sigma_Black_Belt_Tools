One-Way ANOVA

Compare the means of three or more groups with an F-test, effect size, pairwise comparisons and a means chart.

F = MS between ÷ MS within. Pairwise comparisons use a Bonferroni-adjusted t-test on the pooled σ (more conservative than Tukey). Check residuals roughly normal, similar spreads (see the variance tests page) and independent observations. Example: hone-radius readings in µm from three honing stations.
