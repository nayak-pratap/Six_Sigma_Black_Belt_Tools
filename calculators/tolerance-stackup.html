Tolerance Stack-Up and Monte Carlo

Combine component tolerances into an assembly gap with worst-case, root-sum-square and simulated results, and the share of gap variation from each part.

Worst case adds all tolerances: safe but costly. Root-sum-square (RSS) assumes independent, normally distributed parts with tolerance = ±3σ: realistic when many parts contribute. The Monte Carlo simulation draws 20,000 assemblies with the same assumption. Contribution = tolerance² ÷ Σ tolerance². Example: a cartridge pocket assembly (mm) with a gap limit of 0.30 to 0.50.
