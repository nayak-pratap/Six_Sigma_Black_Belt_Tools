Normality Test and Descriptive Statistics

Anderson-Darling normality test, normal probability plot, histogram, skewness, kurtosis and a plain recommendation for what to do next.

The Anderson-Darling statistic compares the sorted data with a fitted normal. A p-value below α suggests non-normality. With large samples tiny departures become significant, so always look at the probability plot: points close to the line mean “normal enough”. Example: 40 hardness readings (HRA) from one sintering lot.
