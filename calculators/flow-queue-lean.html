Takt, Little’s Law, Kanban and Queue Calculator

Lean flow arithmetic in one page: takt time, process cycle efficiency, Little’s Law, kanban card count and a single-server queue.

Takt = available time ÷ demand. PCE = value-added time ÷ lead time (typical factory 1 to 5%; best in class above 25%). Little’s Law: WIP = throughput × lead time, so cutting WIP cuts lead time. Kanban cards = demand × lead time × (1 + safety) ÷ container size. M/M/1 queue: utilisation ρ = λ ÷ μ; average wait = ρ ÷ (μ − λ). Example: a finishing cell.
