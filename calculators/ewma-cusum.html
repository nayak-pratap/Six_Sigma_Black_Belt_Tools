EWMA and CUSUM Charts

Detect small, sustained shifts that Shewhart charts miss, with an EWMA chart and a tabular CUSUM.

EWMA: zᵢ = λxᵢ + (1 − λ)zᵢ₋₁ with limits μ0 ± Lσ√[λ/(2 − λ)·(1 − (1 − λ)²ⁱ)]. CUSUM: C⁺ᵢ = max(0, xᵢ − (μ0 + kσ) + C⁺ᵢ₋₁), C⁻ similarly; signal when either exceeds hσ. Both react faster than a Shewhart chart to a shift of about 0.5σ to 1.5σ. Example: 40 readings with a 0.8σ upward shift from reading 21.
