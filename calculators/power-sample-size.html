Power and Sample Size

How many samples to detect a difference in means or proportions, and what power a given sample really gives.

Power is the chance of detecting a real difference of the stated size. Means: uses the t distribution with a normal approximation of the non-central part (accurate to about one sample). Proportions: normal approximation, so avoid rare events with tiny n. Example: detect a 0.3 µm shift in hone radius when σ = 0.5 µm.
