Acceptance Sampling and OC Curve

Evaluate a single sampling plan (n, c): operating characteristic curve, producer’s and consumer’s risk, and average outgoing quality.

Accept the lot when the sample has c or fewer defectives. Pa(p) = P(X ≤ c) with X binomial(n, p). Producer’s risk α = 1 − Pa(AQL): good lots rejected. Consumer’s risk β = Pa(LTPD): bad lots accepted. AOQ(p) = p·Pa(p)·(N − n) ÷ N with rejected lots 100% screened. Example: n = 120, c = 3, AQL 1%, LTPD 6%.
