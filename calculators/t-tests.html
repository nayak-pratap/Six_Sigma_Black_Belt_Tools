t-Tests: One-Sample, Paired, Two-Sample

Run a one-sample, paired or two-sample (Welch) t-test directly from raw data, with confidence interval and a plain-language verdict.

Paired tests require equal-length lists in the same order. One-sided p-values halve the two-sided p when the sign agrees. The confidence interval is always two-sided at 1 − α. Example: tool life in minutes for two coatings (Welch), which is the default.
