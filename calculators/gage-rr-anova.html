Gage R&R (ANOVA Method)

Analyse a crossed gage study from raw readings: ANOVA with interaction, variance components, %contribution, %study variation and distinct categories.

Crossed design: every operator measures every part, with the same number of repeats. The ANOVA splits variation into part, operator, part × operator and repeatability. If the interaction is not significant (p above 0.25) it is pooled into repeatability. %StudyVar = component σ ÷ total σ. Guide: GRR under 10% good, 10 to 30% marginal, over 30% unacceptable; distinct categories 5 or more. Example: 8 parts, 3 operators, 3 repeats of a width measurement (mm).
