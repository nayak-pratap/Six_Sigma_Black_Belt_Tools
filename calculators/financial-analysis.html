Project Financials: NPV, IRR, Payback, ROI

Turn project savings and costs into NPV, IRR, payback and return on investment, with hard and soft savings kept apart.

Hard savings are cash a finance team will see: lower scrap, fewer hours billed, lower material. Soft savings are real but do not change the budget: freed capacity, avoided cost, better morale. Count soft savings only at a haircut. NPV = Σ cashflowₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ. IRR is the rate that makes NPV zero. Payback is the time until cumulative cash turns positive. Example: tooling and process change costing 180,000 with savings over five years.
