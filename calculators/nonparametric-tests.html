Mann-Whitney and Kruskal-Wallis

Rank-based tests for two groups or for three or more groups when data are not normal, with medians and a verdict.

Two groups: Mann-Whitney (normal approximation with tie correction, fine from about 8 per group). Three or more: Kruskal-Wallis with the chi-square approximation. These compare distributions and medians, make no normality assumption, and are less affected by outliers. Example: skewed repair times in hours for three maintenance crews.
