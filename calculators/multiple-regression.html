Multiple Linear Regression

Fit Y on up to five X variables from pasted columns: coefficients, p-values, R², adjusted R², VIF and residual check.

Model: Y = b0 + b1·X1 + b2·X2 + … Coefficients by least squares. VIF above 5 signals collinearity among the X variables. R² adjusted penalises extra terms. Check the residuals for pattern, which would mean the model is missing something. Example: chipped inserts per 1,000 against sintering temperature, press pressure and powder moisture over 15 batches.
