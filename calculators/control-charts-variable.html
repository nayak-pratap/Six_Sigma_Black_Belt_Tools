Variable Control Charts with Run Rules

X-bar and R chart (or individuals and moving range) from your data, with limits, capability σ and the Western Electric signals flagged.

Limits are set from the data given, so use a baseline period that is in control. X-bar and R: limits use A2, D3, D4. Individuals: limits use 2.66 × MR̄. Rule 2: nine points in a row on one side of the centre line. Rule 3: six in a row steadily rising or falling. A signal means a special cause: investigate; do not adjust for common-cause noise. Example: 20 subgroups of 5 bore diameters with a shift from subgroup 15.
