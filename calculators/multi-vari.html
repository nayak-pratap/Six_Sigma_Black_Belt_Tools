Multi-Vari Analysis

Find the family of variation that matters most: within a piece, between pieces, or over time, from labelled readings.

The multi-vari study samples across time, between parts and within a part, then shows which family drives the spread. For each factor, the page reports the share of total variation explained by its level means (SS between ÷ SS total) and the range of its level means. The biggest share points to where to look first. Example: bore diameter by position along the bore, part within the hour, and hour.
