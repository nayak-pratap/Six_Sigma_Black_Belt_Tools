Attribute Control Charts: p, np, c, u

Control charts for defectives and defects with varying sample sizes: limits for every point and out-of-control flags.

p: p̄ ± 3√(p̄(1−p̄)/n). np: n̄p̄ ± 3√(n̄p̄(1−p̄)). c: c̄ ± 3√c̄. u: ū ± 3√(ū/n). Limits vary with n for p and u charts. Use p and np for pass/fail, c and u for counts of defects where one unit can have several. Example: 24 daily samples of inserts (n varies), with the number rejected for chipping.
