Variance Tests: F, Bartlett and Levene

Test whether group spreads differ, with the right test for normal and non-normal data, and the ratio of standard deviations.

Bartlett’s test is powerful but sensitive to non-normality. Levene (Brown-Forsythe, median-based) is robust and is the safer default. Two groups also get the classic F-test (ratio of variances). Unequal variances do not stop you from using Welch’s t-test, and they matter for ANOVA. Example: bore diameters (mm) from three machines.
