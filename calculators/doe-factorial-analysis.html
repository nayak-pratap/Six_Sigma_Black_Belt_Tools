Two-Level Factorial DOE Analysis

Analyse a 2², 2³ or 2⁴ full factorial: effects, interactions, ANOVA with replicates, Pareto of effects, model equation and best settings.

Standard order means factor A alternates every run, B every two runs, C every four. Effect = (average at high) − (average at low). With replicates the pure-error variance gives F-tests for every term. Without replicates, Lenth’s pseudo standard error flags the large effects. Example: edge strength (N) from a 2³ study of sintering temperature, press pressure and hold time, two replicates.
