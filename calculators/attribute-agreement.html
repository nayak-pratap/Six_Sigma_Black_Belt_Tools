Attribute Agreement (Cohen’s Kappa)

Check whether appraisers agree with each other and with the known standard when judging pass/fail or grades.

Percent agreement ignores agreement by chance. Cohen’s kappa corrects for it: κ = (observed − chance) ÷ (1 − chance). Rule of thumb: above 0.90 excellent, 0.70 to 0.90 acceptable, below 0.70 improve the standard or train the appraisers. Each rater is compared with the standard, and every pair of raters with each other. Example: 30 inserts judged pass (P) or fail (F) by three inspectors against a master panel.
