Weibull Reliability Analysis

Fit a Weibull distribution to failure times: shape and scale, B10 life, mean life and reliability at a mission time.

Median-rank regression: rank F = (i − 0.3) ÷ (n + 0.4); plot ln t against ln(−ln(1 − F)); slope = shape β, scale η = e^(−intercept ÷ β). β below 1: early-life failures; near 1: random failures; above 1: wear-out. B10 = time by which 10% have failed. Reliability R(t) = e^(−(t/η)^β). Example: tool life in minutes of 12 inserts tested to failure.
