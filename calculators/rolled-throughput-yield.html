Rolled Throughput Yield

Combine first-pass yields of each process step into RTY, normalised yield, DPMO and sigma level, and find the weakest step.

First-pass yield (FPY) of a step = good first time ÷ units in. RTY = FPY₁ × FPY₂ × …: the probability a unit passes every step with no rework. Normalised yield = RTY^(1/steps), the average yield per step. A line with five steps at 95% each has an RTY of only 77%. Example: six steps making a coated insert.
