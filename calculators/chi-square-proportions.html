Chi-Square and Proportion Tests

Test independence in a contingency table, or compare two defect proportions, with expected counts, effect size and a verdict.

Chi-square = Σ (observed − expected)² ÷ expected, with expected = row total × column total ÷ grand total, df = (rows − 1)(columns − 1). Expected counts should be at least 5 in most cells. Cramér’s V measures strength (0 to 1). A 2 × 2 table also gives a two-proportion z-test. Example: defects (first column) and good parts (second column) for three shifts.
