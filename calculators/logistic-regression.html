Binary Logistic Regression

Model a pass/fail outcome from one to three predictors: odds ratios, p-values and predicted probability for chosen settings.

ln(p ÷ (1 − p)) = b0 + b1·X1 + … fitted by iteratively reweighted least squares. The odds ratio for X is e^b: the multiple by which the odds of Y = 1 change for a one-unit increase. The G test compares with a model of no predictors. Example: whether an insert fails (1) given its edge-hone radius (µm) and sintering temperature (hundreds of °C above 1,300).
