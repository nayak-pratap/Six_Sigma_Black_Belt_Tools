Stakeholder Engagement and Communication Plan

Map influence and support, spot gaps between where people are and where you need them, and get an engagement action per stakeholder.

For each stakeholder rate influence (1 to 5), current support and the support you need (−2 resistant to +2 champion). The gap drives the engagement action. Example: a chipping-reduction project.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
