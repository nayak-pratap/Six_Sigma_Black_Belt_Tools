QFD House of Quality (Core Matrix)

Weight customer needs against technical requirements to rank which design or process characteristics matter most.

The relationship matrix of the House of Quality: each technical requirement scores the sum of (need importance × relationship strength). The highest scorers are the characteristics to control or design for first. Example: carbide insert needs against engineering characteristics.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
