Change Readiness Audit (ADKAR)

Score awareness, desire, knowledge, ability and reinforcement for a change, and find where people are stuck.

Rate each statement for the people who must change. ADKAR is sequential: a low score early (awareness or desire) blocks everything after. Example: rolling out a new inspection method.

All calculators and toolsWorked examples are illustrative. Check your own data, standards and customer requirements before acting on a result.
