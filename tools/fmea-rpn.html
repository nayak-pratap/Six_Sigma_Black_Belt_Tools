FMEA with RPN and Priority

Score failure modes by severity, occurrence and detection, rank by RPN, and flag high-severity items regardless of RPN.

Score each failure mode 1 to 10 for severity (S), occurrence (O) and detection (D, where 10 means it will not be caught). RPN = S × O × D. A high severity (9 or 10) needs action even when RPN is low. Example: a sintering and grinding process FMEA.
