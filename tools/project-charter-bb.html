Black Belt Project Charter

Draft a full charter with business case, savings, scope, risks and tollgate dates, and get a checklist of what is still weak.

The charter is the contract between the team and the sponsor. Black Belt charters add a quantified business case validated by finance and an explicit risk and resource view. The checks below flag common weaknesses; the output is a clean one-page text to copy. Example: reducing chipped inserts across three lines.
